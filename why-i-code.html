Why do I code?

AI has made me think more about why I code. I was reminiscing at old videos on my phone earlier this week. I saw this video I sent to my CS3500 partner of me getting a tough feature working in Fall 2024. Ecsatatic because 1. there was hope for our grades, and 2. it was fun.

What problems are fun to solve nowadays? I haven't encountered a problem that warrant the power of AI. No ticket at work, no club activity, no startup slop idea conjured up in an hour has felt the same as that fall afternoon. I don't NEED Claude to do what I set my mind out to do, but I fall victim everytime to the siren song of not having to solve the problem myself (and watching YouTube Shorts).

I'm not going all luddite on AI. I want to verbalize how shifitng to this powerful tool has been a campaign on multiple fronts. Am I being overdramatic? Maybe. Should I just embrace the future and start working on agentic workflows to replace myself? Maybe. Is this hand-coded website my spiteful stab at an immovable force; a pitiful attempt to bind myself to the mast? Maybe.
